# Why Building an MVP Can Be a Game-Changer for Your Business 🚀

At RC Web Solutions LLC, we believe that great ideas deserve to be seen — not just talked about.

Recently, a client shared an exciting concept with us: a platform to help families preserve and visualize their history through interactive timelines. Instead of lengthy proposals and endless planning meetings, we took action and built a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to bring the vision to life.

---

## What exactly is an MVP?

An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) is a fully functional version of your application with the essential features needed to validate your idea. It allows you to:

- ✅ See your concept in action before committing to a full-scale project
- ✅ Gather real user feedback early in the process
- ✅ Save time and money by focusing on what truly matters
- ✅ Iterate and improve based on actual data, not assumptions

---

## Here's what we built in record time:

- 🔐 User authentication (Email & Google OAuth)
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family member profiles with photos and biographies
- 📅 Interactive horizontal timeline visualization
- 📁 Document and file storage
- 📱 Fully responsive design (mobile, tablet & desktop)
- ☁️ Production-ready deployment

🔗 **Check out the live demo:** [https://family-tree-web-two.vercel.app](https://family-tree-web-two.vercel.app)

## 🛠️ Tech Stack Used

### Frontend

- ⚛️ Next.js 16 + React 19
- 🎨 TailwindCSS + Shadcn UI
- 📦 Zustand (State Management)
- 🔐 NextAuth.js v5

### Backend

- 🚀 Express.js 5 + TypeScript
- 🗄️ PostgreSQL + Prisma ORM
- ☁️ Cloudinary (Media Storage)

### Infrastructure

- ▲ Vercel (Frontend Hosting)
- 🚂 Railway (Backend & Database)
- 📁 Turborepo (Monorepo Architecture)
