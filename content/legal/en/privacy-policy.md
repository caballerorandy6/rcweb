## YouTube API Services {#youtube}

RC Web Solutions LLC publishes its own videos on its YouTube channel through YouTube API Services. By using Our website You acknowledge that We use YouTube API Services, which are subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google’s handling of data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### What YouTube data We use and store

- **Our own channel only:** the YouTube Data API is used to upload videos to Our channel with Our own Google account. We do not request, collect or store video, channel or account data of any other YouTube user, and no visitor of this website signs in with YouTube.
- **OAuth tokens:** the access and refresh tokens Google issues for Our channel are stored in access-restricted files on Our own private server and workstation, and are used only to upload videos to Our channel.
- **Upload records:** We keep the YouTube video ID of each video We upload so the same video is not published twice. We do not store viewer data, comments or analytics.

### Revoking access and deleting stored data

The authorization granted to Our application can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: [https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Revoking it stops any further use of the YouTube API by Our application.

To ask Us to delete the stored tokens or upload records, email [contactus@rcweb.dev](mailto:contactus@rcweb.dev) with the subject “YouTube data deletion”. We delete the data within **30 days** of the request and confirm by email.
